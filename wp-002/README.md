## Executive Overview

Organizations have invested extensively in enterprise data, Business Intelligence, Artificial Intelligence, predictive analytics, and executive reporting.

Yet producing more intelligence does not automatically produce better decisions.

**Decision Intelligence Engineering** addresses the critical space between enterprise intelligence and executive action.

WP-002 introduces Decision Intelligence Engineering as an enterprise engineering discipline for systematically transforming governed enterprise intelligence into explainable choices, accountable executive commitments, measurable outcomes, and organizational learning.

The publication develops the **Decision Gap**—the persistent gap between what an organization knows and its ability to convert that knowledge into trustworthy executive action.

> **Enterprise intelligence creates value only when it improves the quality of consequential decisions.**

## The Decision Intelligence Engineering Proposition

Decision Intelligence Engineering connects:

**Enterprise Intelligence**

↓

**Evidence & Context**

↓

**Alternatives**

↓

**Governance**

↓

**Explainability**

↓

**Executive Judgment**

↓

**Decision**

↓

**Execution**

↓

**Monitoring**

↓

**Organizational Learning**

The objective is not to automate executive judgment.

It is to engineer the conditions under which human judgment can operate with stronger evidence, clearer alternatives, transparent assumptions, appropriate governance, and measurable accountability.

## Key Contributions

WP-002 develops a structured approach to engineering trustworthy enterprise decisions through several interconnected concepts and frameworks.

### The Decision Gap

Defines the organizational gap between available enterprise intelligence and accountable executive action.

### Enterprise Decision Lifecycle

Establishes a governed lifecycle through which consequential decisions move from evidence and context to alternatives, judgment, commitment, execution, monitoring, and learning.

### Human–AI Decision Collaboration

Defines the complementary roles of Artificial Intelligence and human executive judgment in enterprise decision-making.

### Executive Decision Confidence

Examines the conditions required for leaders to act responsibly under uncertainty.

### Decision Governance

Connects authority, accountability, risk, policy, evidence, and organizational responsibility to the decision process.

### Decision Explainability

Ensures that consequential decisions can be understood, challenged, justified, and reviewed.

### Decision Quality

Extends evaluation beyond the immediate choice to include execution, outcomes, monitoring, and organizational learning.
